This project will develop a RAG-based AI Underwriter to automate DRHP analysis and IPO valuation, replacing manual review of these 300–600 page filings. Literature review draws on Lewis et al. (2020) for core RAG architecture, Cheng et al. (2026) on reranking for financial-report QA, and Dadopoulos et al. (2025) on metadata-driven chunking — informing our retrieval/chunking design for DRHP's inconsistent layouts. For valuation, we reference Katsafados et al. (2020) on ML prediction from prospectus text plus financials, and Singh et al. on ML-driven Indian IPO pricing, our closest benchmark. Sample DRHPs will be collected from SEBI/NSE/BSE filings across sectors as pilot documents. The pipeline will ingest a DRHP, retrieve evidence via RAG, extract/normalize financials, compute ratios deterministically, and link outputs to source for verifiability — alongside comparable-company valuation and ML price-prediction modules generating a recommended price range, benchmarked against actual issue price and listing returns. Work so far: literature review and pipeline planning; implementation next.
